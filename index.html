Rules of the log

▾
  • Format is reps x sets (example: 30 x 10)
  • 2 skips per week max. Preferably none.
  • Check in and log your work every night.
  • 10 sets max for any exercise.
  • 6 sets max for pullups.
  • All sets of an exercise use the same rep count. Add a rep whenever you feel ready.
  • Not a competition — the number is between you and you. The sets are what keep everyone honest.
